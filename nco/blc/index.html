Basic Leader Course

BLC & PRT Drills

A focused reference block for BLC preparation, including the Preparation Drill, Recovery Drill, and the other physical training drills commonly connected to H2F and BLC instruction.

BLC Core Topics

The BLC develops basic leader and trainer skills through the six NCO Common Core Competencies:

  1. Readiness
  2. Leadership
  3. Training Management
  4. Communications
  5. Operations
  6. Program Management

Preparation Drill

The Preparation Drill contains 10 exercises and prepares Soldiers for more demanding physical training. Use this sequence to form and prepare the formation:

  1. Fall in
  2. Extend to the left, march
  3. Arms downward, move
  4. Left face
  5. Extend to the left, march
  6. Arms downward, move
  7. Right face
  8. From front to rear, count off
  9. Even numbers to the left, uncover

At the starting position:

  1. Bend and ReachPosition of attention — “In cadence, exercise”
  2. Rear LungePosition of attention — “In cadence, exercise”
  3. High JumperPosition of attention — “In cadence, exercise”
  4. RowerPosition of attention — “In cadence, exercise”
  5. Squat BenderPosition of attention — “In cadence, exercise”
  6. WindmillPosition of attention — “In cadence, exercise”
  7. Forward LungePosition of attention — “In cadence, exercise”
  8. Prone RowPosition of attention — “In cadence, exercise”
  9. Bent-Leg Body TwistPosition of attention — “In cadence, exercise”
  10. Push-UpPosition of attention — “In cadence, exercise”

At the end:

  1. Assemble to the right, march
  2. Announce what will happen after the formation falls out.
  3. Give the command: “Fall out.”

Recovery Drill

The Recovery Drill contains 8 exercises and is performed after physical training to help return the body toward its pre-exercise state.

  1. Overhead Arm Pull
  2. Rear Lunge
  3. Extend and Flex
  4. Thigh Stretch
  5. Single-Leg Over
  6. Groin Stretch
  7. Calf Stretch
  8. Hamstring Stretch

Conditioning Drill 1

  1. Power Jump
  2. V-Up
  3. Mountain Climber
  4. Leg Tuck and Twist
  5. Single-Leg Push-Up

Conditioning Drill 2

  1. Turn and Lunge
  2. Supine Bicycle
  3. Half Jack
  4. Swimmer
  5. 8-Count T Push-Up
