Basic Leader Course

BLC & PRT Drills

A focused reference block for BLC preparation, including the Preparation Drill, Recovery Drill, and the other physical training drills commonly connected to H2F and BLC instruction.

BLC Core Topics

The BLC develops basic leader and trainer skills through the six NCO Common Core Competencies:

  1. Readiness
  2. Leadership
  3. Training Management
  4. Communications
  5. Operations
  6. Program Management

Preparation Drill

The Preparation Drill contains 10 exercises and prepares Soldiers for more demanding physical training.

  1. Bend and Reach
  2. Rear Lunge
  3. High Jumper
  4. Rower
  5. Squat Bender
  6. Windmill
  7. Forward Lunge
  8. Prone Row
  9. Bent-Leg Body Twist
  10. Push-Up

Recovery Drill

The Recovery Drill contains 8 exercises and is performed after physical training to help return the body toward its pre-exercise state.

  1. Overhead Arm Pull
  2. Rear Lunge
  3. Extend and Flex
  4. Thigh Stretch
  5. Single-Leg Over
  6. Groin Stretch
  7. Calf Stretch
  8. Hamstring Stretch
